WHAT USED TO BE HERE AND IS NOT ANY MORE
----------------------------------------
Python 3.12, OCRmyPDF, pikepdf, pypdfium2, Pillow, lxml, fpdf2, img2pdf and
Tcl/Tk were bundled while the OCR ran through OCRmyPDF. This branch drives
tesseract.exe and gswin64c.exe directly from PowerShell/.NET, so none of them
is shipped, and their licence files were removed with them. They are still in
this repository's history, and on the `main` and `windows-native-gui` branches.

THE SCRIPTS WRITTEN FOR THIS PROJECT
------------------------------------
START OCR.bat, Document OCR.exe, everything under system\, and build_bundle.sh
are released under the MIT licence. The text is in LICENSE at the top of this folder.

These scripts call the bundled programs as separate programs. They do not
modify them and are not derived from them.
